Your five questions from running the card on real campaigns

All five answers were decided by Julia on Oct 6. Every card below is drawn by the same rule as the study, with example days.

1. A past week on the overview card

You asked: the first draft came in on day 44, so the card shows a week that is over, with a grey line. The overview card has no line. Is a past week OK there?

No. Once the week has passed, the overview card shows the card's grey line instead of the date.
  • When: before the first post is live, once today's week is later than the week on the card, in the brand's time zone.
  • What: "first content expected the week of ..." is replaced by whichever grey line the card shows, without its final period.
  • Ends: once the first post is live, the overview goes back to "3 of 10 live · first on ...".

2. Long tails

You asked: past week 8 with posts missing (day 91, 2 of 5 live), the bar is full and nothing is marked Now. Is that right?

The bar stays full, but Post stays marked Now while posts are missing and the campaign is not complete.
  • Collapsed: "Post" in the current-phase style (dark text, weight 600); the other four muted. The line under the date is unchanged: "2 of 5 posts live."
  • Expanded: the Post row keeps the filled dot and the Now pill, dates unchanged. The "After week 8" row is also marked Now.
  • Nothing else: no count of what is missing, no amber, no change to the overview card.
  • Ends: once the campaign is complete, Post goes back to the done style and the "After week 8" row disappears.

3. Campaigns that never complete

You asked: every post has been live since May or June, and the campaigns are still active. Should someone complete them?

Yes, automatically. A campaign completes on its own 7 days after every post is live, on top of today's two ways (every creator thanked, or by hand).
  • A campaign with posts still missing never completes on its own (see point 2).
  • Thank-yous stay possible after completion.
  • The campaigns you found complete the day this ships.

4. Stalled campaigns

You asked: 129 days in creator selection with no creators, and the date moves a day later every day. Cap it, or hide it?

Cap it at 4 weeks. Once the brand is more than 4 weeks past one of its steps, the card stops showing a date and says what first content waits for.
  • Creators not approved: "5 weeks after you approve your creators"
  • Brand ships its own orders: "3 weeks after the first order is delivered"
  • Brand reviews its own drafts: "Once you approve the first draft"
  • The amber line stays under it. No crossed-out date. The overview card shows the amber line in place of the date, as in point 1: "Week 6 of 8 · 5 creators are ready for your review".
  • From 6 weeks past the step, the overview card drops the week number too and shows only the amber line: "5 creators are ready for your review".
  • Flag the campaign to Katie's team at 4 weeks.

5. The completion date

You asked: 6 campaigns were completed in one batch on Oct 1, but their last posts went live weeks earlier. Which date should the card show?

No date. The card says "Campaign complete" with the post count under it, and the overview reads "5 live · campaign complete". A campaign completed with no post live shows "Campaign complete" and nothing else.
